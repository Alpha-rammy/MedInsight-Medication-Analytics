📖 Project Overview
Medication utilisation and expenditure are important considerations in healthcare resource planning, medicines optimisation, and population health management.

In this project, I analysed synthetic Synthea healthcare data to understand how recorded medication activity and expenditure vary across patient groups and over time.

Rather than simply building a dashboard, I wanted to answer the following business question:

How does medication utilisation and expenditure vary across patient groups over time, and which patterns warrant further investigation?

A key consideration throughout this project was recognising that higher medication utilisation does not automatically indicate inappropriate prescribing or inefficient healthcare spending.

Differences in utilisation may reflect:

Chronic disease burden
Treatment complexity
Multiple concurrent conditions
Age-related healthcare needs
Long-term therapies
I therefore approached the dashboard as a decision-support tool that highlights patterns for investigation rather than judging the appropriateness of treatment.

🔍 Understanding the Data Grain
One of the first questions I asked was:

What does one row represent?

FactMedication
One row represents:

One recorded medication episode

A patient can therefore appear multiple times because they may have multiple medication episodes.

DimPatient
One row represents:

One unique patient

Understanding this distinction was important because counting medication rows does not provide the number of unique patients.

To measure the patient population accurately, I used DISTINCTCOUNT() rather than counting rows.

Total Patients
Total Patients =

Total Medication Episodes
Total Medication Episodes =
COUNTROWS(FactMedication)
Total Medication Cost
Total Medication Cost =
SUM(FactMedication[TOTALCOST])
Average Cost Per Patient
Average Cost per Patient =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Medication Cost],
    [Total Patients],
    0

3. High Utilisation Was Common

4. Costs Varied Significantly Between Segments
Average expenditure increased substantially between Low, Medium, and High Utilisation groups.

5. Expenditure Varied Over Time
Monthly, annual, and rolling analyses revealed notable expenditure fluctuations across reporting periods.

🔒 Data Governance & Privacy Considerations
This project uses synthetic Synthea healthcare data, meaning no real patient information was processed.

If I were developing this solution using real patient data within an NHS or HIPAA-regulated environment, I would apply additional governance, privacy, and security controls.

These would include:

Data Minimisation
Accessing only the data required to answer specific analytical questions.

De-identification & Pseudonymisation
Removing or pseudonymising direct patient identifiers prior to analysis.

Role-Based Access Controls
Restricting access according to job role and legitimate business need.

Secure Analytics Environments
Performing analysis within approved environments that include encryption, audit logging, and monitoring controls.

Regulatory Compliance
Ensuring compliance with relevant frameworks including:

UK GDPR
Data Protection Act 2018
NHS Data Security & Protection Toolkit
Caldicott Principles
HIPAA safeguards where applicable
Disclosure Controls
Applying suppression, aggregation, and disclosure-control techniques to minimise re-identification risk when reporting small patient cohorts.

This helps ensure analytics remain secure, compliant, and ethically responsible.

⚠️ Limitations
Synthetic Data
This project uses synthetic Synthea healthcare data and does not represent any real healthcare organisation.

Descriptive Analysis
The dashboard identifies patterns and associations but does not establish causal relationships.

Limited Clinical Adjustment
The analysis does not fully adjust for:

Disease severity
Comorbidities
Treatment complexity
Cost Interpretation
Recorded medication expenditure should not be interpreted as real-world savings opportunities without additional clinical and financial validation.
